Methodology & how to read this

The map's own score for each of 87 economies: Σ (import share × supplier HHI × 1/(1+σ)) over ~170 HS-3 sectors, ×1000, core basis (excluding HS ch. 71 / gold), how hard that economy's total import basket would be to replace if a supplier relationship broke, combining how much of its trade sits in concentrated sectors (supplier HHI) with how substitutable those suppliers are (Broda–Greenfield–Weinstein σ). It is not a measure of alignment or bilateral exposure to any one partner: a country can score low here while depending entirely on one geopolitically hostile supplier, as long as that supplier is easy to substitute for the economy as a whole.

The "Show Türkiye's connections" overlay is a different, simpler metric layered on top: for each of 56 tracked partners, Türkiye's own import share from that partner against that partner's share of Türkiye's import-risk index, both already summable pieces of the same underlying index computation. A partner's multiplier (risk share ÷ import share) above 1 means it contributes more to Türkiye's risk than its trade volume alone would suggest.

Source: data/panel_hs92_1995_2024.csv, BACI (CEPII) version V202601, 1995–2024, computed at HS-3 with country-specific σ where available and a common-σ estimate otherwise (marked in the tooltip, see methodology decision D10).
